THE PIPELINE

      
HOW TERMIART TURNS PIXELS INTO LIVING ART

      
Four deliberate stages of creative technology running locally on your machine.


      

        

          
01 // INGEST

          
Aspect-Correct Scaling

          
Terminal monospace fonts are taller than wide (~2:1 aspect ratio). Our engine pre-calculates exact character geometry to prevent image squishing.

        


        

          
02 // ENHANCE

          
Contrast & Sobel Kernels

          
Pillow convolutions detect high-contrast edge gradients, separating foreground figures from dark backdrops for crisp character readability.

        


        

          
03 // RENDER

          
Subpixel Half-Blocks & Braille

          
Using Unicode characters (▀▄ and Braille matrices), we pack 2 to 8 subpixels into every single character space with 16.7M TrueColor ANSI codes.

        


        

          
04 // SHARE

          
Universal Format Export

          
Instant clipboard export for Discord code blocks, high-resolution PNG terminal snapshots for WhatsApp and X, and raw ANSI files for hackers.
